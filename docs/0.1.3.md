# KWAMSTER 0.1.3: combat intelligence, detection accuracy and analytics visualization

Starting commit: `977f1ffe5c76a39a7ac0a8af85dc53475ae10d91` on `codex/0.1.2-combat-corrections`, clean tree. The work is committed locally on the same branch; the completion report records the ending SHA. Nothing was pushed, published, installed into the owner's Aurora instance, or uploaded. Starting state, tracked and ignored inventories, and SHA-256 digests of every file under the instance's `kwamster/` and `logs/` were saved in the ignored `.local-diagnostics/v013/`.

The investigation that drove this release is in [MEASUREMENT_AUDIT.md](MEASUREMENT_AUDIT.md). Metric definitions are in [METRICS.md](METRICS.md), and the design in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 1. Root causes found

- **No 0.1.2 recording was a win or loss.** Of 170 recordings, 115 were Interrupted and 55 Unknown. The causes:
  - The Minemen adapter matched only `minemen.net`; the owner plays on `minemen.club`.
  - Minemen `<Mode> Duel` start blocks were unrecognized.
  - PVPHQ one-name winner lines were rejected 70 times because 0.1.2 demanded a death that PVPHQ never shows.
  - PVPHQ's kill, loser and match-report lines and Minemen round lines were ignored.
  - Best-of rounds dropped the announced opponent.
- **No local death in 222 recordings.** Duel servers respawn players before a death is visible on a client tick.
- **Unattributed damage treated as retaliation.** 652 incoming events without a cause entity counted as opponent hits; 240 of them broke a combo.
- **Shield blocks counted as uncertain.** In 1.21.11 a blocked hit sends no damage event, only a sound, so blocked attacks lowered accuracy.
- **Momentum was overconfident.** It was drawn as a confident curve even when the opponent's side was unobservable.

## 2. Kill, death, win and loss detection

- Adapters for `minemen.net` and `minemen.club`:
  - every `<Mode> Duel` and `Casual Match` start block;
  - round lines (`PlayerA won! 1 - 0`, with optional health);
  - paired match results, including spectator suffixes;
  - kill messages;
  - `Match started!`.
- PVPHQ adapters: opponent line, ranked/unranked banner, `has been killed by`, `MATCH REPORT`, and winner/loser lines with best-of statistics. Winner/loser lines are round results unless they directly follow `MATCH REPORT`.
- Explicit `Resolution`:
  - **Classification:** confirmed server-declared win or loss, inferred win or loss, draw, unknown, interrupted.
  - **Kept separately:** source, confidence, scope (match, round or engagement), the establishing announcement index or event sequence, and notes.
- A kill message is an inferred result, distinct from a declared victory.
  - It is upgraded when the server's result follows within the grace period.
  - A kill by a third party is Unknown, never a win.
  - Contradictions of equal strength become Unknown.
- **Rematches:** a rematch is ambiguous only when the previous round against that opponent was unresolved. In the owner's logs the next Minemen Duel block arrives one second after the result, and those rematches are now confirmed.
- **Delayed results:**
  - A new start while an unresolved fight runs is deferred for up to 2 s, so a late result attaches to the round it belongs to.
  - Forfeits during the countdown are accepted.
  - A round result waits 8 s for the match result that usually follows.
- **Server-side endpoints:**
  - The local combat-kill packet is captured.
  - Opponent removal followed by a new target is Unknown rather than Interrupted.
  - Teleports are recorded but never end a fight.
  - Titles are kept as non-authoritative evidence.
- **Finalization happens exactly once.** Announced rounds with no combat and no result are not recorded, and `Round 1 started!` after a match block continues the same recording.

**Labeled fixture evaluation.** `scripts/extract_outcome_fixtures.py` reads the owner's logs read-only and writes 1,200 anonymized match fixtures to `src/test/resources/fixtures/outcomes.json`. The anonymizer was checked for leaks: no known player name and no unexpected token remains. Labels come from an independent consensus rule in the script. `OutcomeFixtureTest` replays them through the real adapters and engine with simulated melee. ## 3. Incoming hits and shields

- **Incoming damage** records:
  - attribution: opponent melee, opponent indirect, other entity or unattributed;
  - the damage type;
  - ground, sprint and movement state;
  - the observed health drop within two ticks;
  - knockback velocity within one tick;
  - any server crit animation on you.
- **What counts as an opponent hit.** Only attributed opponent hits break combos, move dominance and pair with jump resets. Third-party and unattributed damage is recorded, never discarded or misattributed.
- **Shield blocks.** The attack records whether the target was visibly blocking and facing you. Shield-block and shield-break sounds near either participant are observed.
  - A confirmed block needs a blocking target, no damage event and the block sound.
  - A blocking, facing target with neither is an estimated block, and it stays uncertain.
  - Confirmed blocks leave the accuracy denominator: accuracy = hits / (attempts − confirmed blocks).
- **Real client confirmation.** In the Fabric client test, a real off-hand shield block produced no damage event and one block sound, which is exactly the bytecode analysis.

## 4. Combos and dominance

`combat.metrics.Combos` is shared by the engine and analytics. Combos are consecutive confirmed hits without a confirmed reply. A same-tick trade extends neither side. Disengagement over the combo timeout segments exchanges. Duplicate observations count once. The longest combo, combo count, hits in combos, durations and start/end times, the longest unanswered streak and per-exchange advantage are all derived. Opponent combos are marked estimated or unavailable according to attribution.

Dominance is D = (P − N) / (P + N + 1) over inputs decaying with a 3 s time constant:

- Hits weigh 1, or 1.25 when they continue a combo.
- Shield events weigh 0.25–0.5.
- Accuracy, aim and reach are not inputs.

No curve is drawn without evidence, unattributed damage is shown dotted and neutral, and time shares exclude inactive and incomplete time. METRICS.md has the complete model.

## 5. Jump resets, reach, timing and aim

- **Jump resets.** Every attributed incoming hit gets a category: same tick, within the window, outside, no jump, excluded movement state, or unavailable. Signed offsets, movement state and knockback are attached. An experimental speed-after-knockback comparison appears only with three or more samples per group, and success is never reported.
- **Reach:**
  - eligible hits (confirmed with ray contact);
  - share within the configured window;
  - consistency (SD), average deviation from the target, maximum reach and per-weapon statistics;
  - a 0.05-block histogram with the shaded window. Raw values are preserved.
- **Timing:**
  - per-weapon samples of interval minus expected cooldown, with the outcome of each attempt;
  - resets marked;
  - a new **below resolution** grade for cooldowns not longer than the tick tolerance;
  - no pooled raw averages across weapons.
- **Aim:**
  - contacted face and distance from the ideal point;
  - central share and consistency;
  - breakdowns by weapon and by reach band;
  - measured coordinates kept separate from the derived grade.

## 6. GUI and HTML

The dashboard keeps its visual identity, with six tabs:

| Tab | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | — |
| Offense | Accuracy, Reach & spacing, Aim, Weapon timing, Critical hits, Sprint hits |
| Defense | Incoming hits, Shields, Jump resets, Damage & knockback |
| Fight Flow | Combos, Dominance, Exchanges (focused fight with Older / Newer, selectable combos) |
| History | — |
| Settings | General, Controls, Sounds, Measurement, Recording & storage, Interface |

New in-game charts: combo timeline, dominance graph, reach histogram, timing scatter and jump-offset strip. Weapon and attempt filters are chips. Every chart has units, a legend, tooltips and an empty state. Deterministic interpretations appear on the Overview, in History details and in Exchanges.

The report (payload format 4) has five tabs: Overview, Offense, Defense, Fight Flow and Timeline. It adds:

- a clickable combo timeline with an event table;
- the dominance graph, with match boundaries, hit and combo markers, and a state strip;
- the reach histogram with a weapon filter and per-hit strip;
- an aim face view with weapon and segment filters, where dots are measured and colors are derived;
- the timing scatter with weapon and result filters;
- a shield timeline;
- a zoomable tick-aligned jump timeline.

All of it is built with `textContent` and SVG, has no network code, and keeps the original content security policy.

## 9. Remaining limitations

- Only Minemen and PVPHQ formats come from evidence. Other servers rely on conservative generic rules and stay inferred.
- Results arriving more than the grace period late are not attached.
- An opponent's shield block could not be produced in the singleplayer test (mobs do not raise shields). It is covered by unit and simulated tests only.
- Damage dealt, knockback reduction and jump-reset success remain unavailable.
- No manual two-player multiplayer session was performed in this assignment.
- Analytics now cost more per fight than in 0.1.2 (see PERFORMANCE.md). The cost is computed on history change, not per frame.
