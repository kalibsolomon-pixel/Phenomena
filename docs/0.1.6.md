## Independent combat measurement

`CombatExchange` opens on a unique eligible aimed attempt (including an opening miss), a correlated outgoing hit, or an incoming hit reliably attributed to the opponent. It ends after **3.0 seconds** without a qualifying interaction, configurable independently over 0.5–30 seconds. The class has no recording-finalization callback. Explicit round boundaries also segment correlation and exchanges. A single recorded match can contain many intervals and breaks.

The threshold uses monotonic elapsed nanoseconds, checked on client events/ticks. Stalls may delay processing, but endpoints are clamped to the last qualifying interaction plus the threshold. An integrated-server pause can end a measurement exchange; configured recording/manual parents continue. The generic recording timeout remains separate and can still expire under its existing monotonic policy. Changing the exchange setting applies at the next update; previous interval endpoints remain frozen.

Attack input hooks remain vanilla entity attack calls and validated air swings. An air swing can establish opening intent when its ray intersects an opponent box expanded by 0.35 blocks within six blocks and the opponent is visible/valid for the current match. This bounded query occurs only at attack time. Arbitrary empty-space swings, attacks at unrelated configured-match participants, weapon holding, environmental damage and uncorrelated defensive sounds do not open or extend an exchange. This is an explicit client geometry heuristic, not proof of intent or a server acceptance verdict. Spears' separate piercing/jabbing path and other nonstandard server damage sources remain outside validated melee coverage.

`AttackObservations` consolidates local outgoing observations before classification, timing, reach, hit feedback or combo aggregation. The window is **inclusive two client ticks**, anchored to the first observation. 100/102 become one; 104 starts another. Different targets or weapons inside the owner's window retain their distinct raw observations while sharing one reported attempt. Hit confirmation must match the canonical selected target; conflicting rapid target evidence is retained and can remain unresolved rather than borrowing another target's reach. Incoming attacks, other actors and independent shield events are never folded into the outgoing-input group. Missing tick indices are retained separately; wall-clock milliseconds do not substitute for ticks.

Frozen maps retain raw-to-canonical IDs, selected sample IDs, classifications and canonical timing. Duplicate observations do not increment attempts/misses/hits, extend combos or intervals, or repeat perfect-spacing feedback. Raw events remain intact. A duplicate that supplies the first valid target can refine an excluded/unresolved canonical input. Eligible aimed misses wait for the bounded correlation window (default 250 ms, configurable 50–500 ms). Direct entity calls without confirmation remain unresolved. A correlated shield sound is tracked separately; blocking/facing state alone is uncertain. Damage arriving within the window can supersede a prior ambiguous shield correlation, yielding one final hit evaluation and one feedback event.

| Metric | 0.1.6 definition |
| --- | --- |
| Total swings | All observed local outgoing attack inputs during the recording, before semantic classification; not mining or unobserved input paths |
| Unique attacks | Canonical groups after the two-tick rule |
| Eligible attempts | Unique attacks with relevant opponent targeting/combat evidence |
| Confirmed hits | Eligible canonical attempts with correlated local melee-source damage |
| Eligible misses | Aimed empty swings without confirmation after the bounded window; inferred misses, never every left click |
| Confirmed shield interactions | Correlated opponent block/disable sound; attribution remains inferred from client locality/correlation |
| Uncertain shields | Blocking/facing evidence without a correlated sound; subset of uncertainty |
| Excluded swings | Noncombat/unrelated unique inputs; outside the accuracy denominator |
| Unresolved | Insufficient outcome evidence, separately exposed from uncertain shields; never automatically a miss |
| Combat accuracy | hits / (eligible attempts − confirmed shield blocks); unresolved and uncertain shields remain in the denominator to avoid inflation |

The counters satisfy total inputs = unique attacks + duplicate observations, and unique attacks = eligible attempts + excluded swings. Eligible attempts partition into hits, eligible misses, confirmed shields, uncertain shields and other unresolved attacks. When no eligible samples exist, the percentage displays unavailable. Historical schemas keep their original counts and policy; missing new categories display unavailable rather than fabricated zero measurements.

Confirmed retaliation, same-tick trades and existing combo timeout still segment combos. Exchange/round boundaries additionally split combo continuity and reset momentum. Unanswered hit streaks retain their distinct gap-ignoring definition; they are not presented as continuous exchange combos. Dominance keeps its existing documented evidence weights and decay, with resets and unavailable samples outside measured exchanges. Missing incoming attribution never fabricates opponent advantage.
